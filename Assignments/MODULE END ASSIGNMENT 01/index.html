Bridging Borders and Framing Moments: Global Admissions & Creative Imagery

With a professional background in international student admissions, I specialize in connecting students from diverse cultural and academic backgrounds with life-changing educational opportunities. My role involves guiding applicants through complex admission processes with clarity, cultural awareness, and a deep understanding of global education systems. From initial outreach to enrollment, I aim to make every student's transition smooth, personalized, and successful.

Parallel to my administrative work, I am also a passionate photographer, capturing the world’s stories through a creative lens.
